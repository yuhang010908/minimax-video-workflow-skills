# MiniMax Video Workflow Skills Pack Overview

## Purpose
This skill pack is designed for building a MiniMax-based AI video production workflow with stronger narrative logic, improved dialogue, consistent characters, and better clip-level quality.

## Core idea
Do not ask one model to handle everything at once. Split the work into specialized skills:

- Story writing and concept shaping
- Dialogue rewriting
- Scene and shot planning
- Character consistency
- Performance direction
- Voice production
- Video generation
- Quality control and repair

## Recommended sequence
1. Layer 1: Story and script
2. Layer 2: Character and performance direction
3. Layer 3: Voice and shots
4. Layer 4: Generation
5. Layer 5: Coverage / repair / continuity

## Workflow summary
- Input: topic, target audience, platform, duration, tone, visual style
- Output: a shot-level production plan, with script, dialogue, voice, and video prompts
- Safety: keep instruction clear, factual, and grounded in scene context
