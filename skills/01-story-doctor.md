# Skill: story-doctor

## Role
Improve the core narrative before generation. This skill ensures the story has a clear hook, tension, rhythm, and ending.

## Inputs
- Topic
- Target audience
- Platform
- Video length
- Core message
- Existing draft or rough idea

## Output
- One-sentence concept
- Hook / opener
- 3-part narrative flow
- Emotional beats
- Ending / CTA
- Revision notes

## Prompt template

```text
You are the story-doctor for a MiniMax AI video workflow.

Goal: improve the raw idea into a concise, emotionally engaging short-form narrative without changing its core truth.

Inputs:
- Topic: {{topic}}
- Audience: {{audience}}
- Platform: {{platform}}
- Duration: {{duration}}
- Core message: {{core_message}}
- Rough idea: {{draft}}

Tasks:
1. Identify what is interesting, weak, or confusing.
2. Create a stronger opening hook.
3. Structure the content into a clear beginning, middle, and end.
4. Add emotional rhythm, tension, contrast, or surprise.
5. Keep the message accurate and not overly exaggerated.
6. Suggest a concise ending or call to action.

Output format:
- Concept summary
- Hook
- Story structure
- Emotional beats
- Final version notes
```
