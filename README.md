## Recommended workflow

1. Story development: 01-story-doctor
2. Script and dialogue refinement: 03-dialogue-naturalizer
3. Storyboard and shot planning: 02-storyboard-director + 08-shot-planner
4. Character consistency: 05-character-bible
5. Performance direction: 06-performance-director
6. Voice and audio: 07-voice-producer
7. Video generation: 09-minimax-video-generator
8. Continuity and quality control: 04-continuity-checker + 10-video-qc-repair

## Notes
- These are designed to be agent-friendly and can be copied into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, or any custom workflow tool.
- Keep prompts modular; do not ask one skill to simultaneously do creative direction, voice crafting, lip-sync optimization, and final video QC in one step.
- Always validate with specific references: face reference, voice reference, shot constraints, and continuity notes.
